Cognitive Atlas

Turn what you're learning into a city you can walk through.

Most notes are flat. You write them once, scroll past them twice, and forget where anything lives. Cognitive Atlas gives your knowledge a place instead of a page — a navigable landscape where every idea has an address, every relationship is a road you can trace, and every subject is a skyline you built yourself.

Drop a study block for each concept and watch it become a building. Link two ideas and a road appears between them. Group a unit under a shared district of grass, cross it with a river, mark your hardest topic with a lit tower you can spot from anywhere in the city. This is a visualization matrix for everything you're trying to remember — except instead of scrolling, you navigate. Spatial memory does the work your highlighter never could.
